25 Things to Do When You're Bored as a Student

Sometimes you have a completely free afternoon and absolutely no idea what to do with it. Here are 25 simple ideas for having fun, trying something new, getting things done or making an ordinary day a little more interesting.

1. Go for a Walk Without a Plan

Put your headphones on, leave your room and just walk. You don't need a destination. Explore a street you've never walked down or find a quiet place to sit for a while.

2. Make a New Playlist

Make a playlist for a specific mood, season or part of your life. You could create one for late-night studying, weekend mornings, travelling or simply songs you never want to forget.

3. Rearrange Your Room

You don't need to buy anything. Move your desk, change where you keep your books or simply reorganize a shelf. A small change can make your room feel completely different.

4. Try a New Recipe

Find something you've never cooked before and give it a try. It can be something simple like pasta, pancakes, cookies or a new drink.

5. Have a No-Phone Hour

Put your phone somewhere you can't immediately reach it and spend an hour doing something else. Read, draw, cook, clean or simply sit outside.

6. Visit Somewhere You've Never Been

You don't need to travel far. Find a café, park, library, bookstore or neighbourhood you've never explored and make an afternoon out of it.

7. Make a List of Places You Want to Visit

Start a travel list. It can include cities you've always wanted to visit, weekend destinations or even places near you that you've never explored.

8. Clean Out Your Phone

Delete screenshots, old downloads and apps you don't use. Organize your photos into albums and clean up your home screen.

9. Learn Something Completely Random

Pick a topic you know almost nothing about and spend 30 minutes learning about it. It could be architecture, astronomy, cooking, languages, history or anything else that catches your attention.

10. Start a Journal

You don't have to write pages every day. Start with a few sentences about what happened today, what you're thinking about or something you're looking forward to.

11. Call Someone You Haven't Spoken to Recently

Think of someone you haven't talked to in a while and send them a message or give them a call. Sometimes a simple conversation can completely change your day.

12. Make a Mini Bucket List

Write down ten things you want to do this year. Keep them realistic and fun — places to visit, foods to try, skills to learn or experiences you want to have.

13. Go to a Bookstore

Walk around a bookstore without necessarily planning to buy anything. Pick up books you've never heard of and see what catches your attention.

14. Take Yourself on a Solo Coffee Date

Find a café, order something you like and bring a book, journal or headphones. Spending time alone can actually be pretty enjoyable.

15. Make a Mood Board

Create a digital or physical mood board for the life you want this year. Add places, outfits, rooms, hobbies, quotes, colours and anything else that inspires you.

16. Try a New Hobby

Try something you've always been curious about. Photography, drawing, baking, dancing, reading, coding, photography, playing an instrument or learning a language are all places to start.

17. Have a Movie Night

Pick a movie you've been meaning to watch, make some snacks and turn your evening into a proper little event.

18. Explore Your Campus

Find a building, study space, café, garden or quiet corner you've never visited. You might discover a new favourite place.

19. Make a Vision Board for Your Semester

Think about what you want the next few months to feel like. Add goals, places you want to go, habits you want to build and experiences you want to have.

20. Organize Your Digital Life

Clean your laptop, organize your folders and rename files properly. It might not sound exciting, but future-you will appreciate it.

21. Have a Friends Cooking Night

Invite a few friends over and cook something together. It doesn't have to be complicated — the point is spending time together.

22. Take Photos Around Your City

Take your phone or camera outside and look at your surroundings differently. Photograph buildings, signs, streets, food, sunsets or small details you normally walk past.

23. Plan Your Perfect Weekend

Even if you aren't actually going anywhere, plan a dream weekend. Choose where you'd go, what you'd eat, what you'd see and who you'd spend it with.

24. Do Something You've Been Putting Off

Pick one small task you've been avoiding and finally do it. Reply to the message, clean the drawer, submit the form or organize the notes. Getting it done feels better than thinking about it.

25. Do Absolutely Nothing

Being bored isn't always a problem that needs fixing. Sometimes you can simply lie down, listen to music, look out the window and let your brain rest.

Pick One and Go

You don't need to completely transform your day. Pick one idea from this list and try it.

Sometimes the best plans are the ones you make five minutes before leaving your room.

Pick a mood

Need to get out?
Go for a walk, explore somewhere new or take yourself on a coffee date.

Want to stay in?
Rearrange your room, make a playlist, cook something or watch a movie.

Want to be productive?
Organize your phone, clean your digital files or finish something you've been putting off.

Remember

You don't have to make every free moment productive. Sometimes doing something just because you enjoy it is enough.
