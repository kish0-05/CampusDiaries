Study Abroad Essentials: The Ultimate University Moving Checklist

Moving abroad for university is exciting, but there is a lot to remember before you leave. From important documents and luggage to technology, clothes, money and those small things you only realise you need once you're already there — this checklist is designed to help you get organised before your new chapter begins.

How to use this checklist:

Work through each section before your move and tick things off as you prepare them. You don't need to buy everything on this list — use it as a starting point and adjust it depending on your destination, university and lifestyle.

1. Important Documents

Your documents should be one of the first things you organise. Keep important originals somewhere safe and keep digital copies in a secure place as well.

2. Your Travel Backpack

Keep the things you need during the journey in your carry-on rather than putting everything into your checked luggage.

🎒 What to look for in a student backpack

Look for something comfortable, durable and large enough for your laptop, documents and everyday university essentials.

3. Luggage

You don't need to take your entire room with you. Focus on the things you'll actually use during your first few weeks.

🧳 Packing tip

Packing cubes can make it much easier to separate clothes, accessories and other small items inside your suitcase.

See luggage options →

4. Shoes

Shoes can take up a surprising amount of luggage space, so choose a few pairs that actually work for your everyday life.

👟 Your everyday pair matters

If you'll be walking or using public transport frequently, comfortable shoes are one of the things you'll probably use almost every day.

See comfortable shoe options →

5. Money & Banking

Sort out your payment options before you travel so you aren't trying to figure everything out during your first few days.

6. Laptop & Technology

Your technology will probably become a big part of university life, so check that everything is working before you leave.

7. Travel Adapter

Check which plug type your destination uses before you travel. An adapter can be one of those tiny things you really don't want to realise you forgot after arriving.

🔌 A useful travel essential

A universal travel adapter can be useful if you expect to travel to different countries during your studies.

See travel adapter options →

8. Clothes

Think about the climate where you'll actually be living rather than packing for every possible situation.

9. Toiletries

You can buy many toiletries after arriving, so focus on the things you personally need immediately.

10. Medication & Health

If you take regular medication, check the rules for bringing it into your destination country and carry the appropriate documentation.

11. Student Room Essentials

Don't pack everything for your future room. Check what your accommodation already provides first.

12. Reusable Water Bottle

A reusable bottle is a small thing that can become part of your everyday university routine.

💧 Everyday student essential

Look for a bottle that's easy to carry and clean, especially if you'll be walking around campus throughout the day.

15. Things You Probably Don't Need to Pack

One of the easiest ways to overpack is trying to bring everything from home. Before putting something in your suitcase, ask: Can I easily buy this after I arrive?

Depending on your destination and accommodation, you may not need to bring large quantities of toiletries, kitchen supplies, cleaning products, stationery or bulky room decorations.

16. Your First-Week Essentials

The first few days are mostly about getting settled. Keep your most important things easy to access.

Final Thought

You don't need to have every single detail figured out before you leave. The goal isn't to pack your entire life into a suitcase — it's to make the first few days in your new country a little easier.

Pack the essentials, organise your important documents, leave some room in your suitcase and give yourself time to settle in. Your university chapter is about to begin.
